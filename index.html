The problem

In the first Belgian study on digital violence in relationships, 39% of people said their online activity had been checked or monitored without their consent.

Caseworkers see it every week, but have no time or tools to fix it.

IGVM/IEFH, Digital violence in dating and partner relationships in Belgium (Universiteit Antwerpen, UGent, ULB), January 2026.

How it works

Three steps, one appointment.

  1. 1

    Scan the phone

    Check for stalkerware in about 10 minutes. No IT skills needed.

  2. 2

    Order a clean phone

    A certified refurbished phone, set up with new accounts.

  3. 3

    Move over and save evidence

    Photos and contacts are moved and evidence is saved, all in one appointment.
